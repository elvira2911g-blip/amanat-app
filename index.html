Amanat

Amanat is a personal, local automation assistant built and used by a single individual for their own private tasks. It is not a commercial product and is not distributed to other users.

One of its functions is read-only access to the owner's own Gmail account (scope gmail.readonly), used to search and read the owner's own correspondence as part of personal workflow automation running entirely on the owner's own computer.
